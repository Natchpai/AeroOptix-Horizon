## 1. Project Definition

### 1.1 Purpose and application

A custom PX4 flight controller hardware platform with a dual-MCU architecture. The STM32H743VIT6 is the main flight-controller MCU and drives the 4 tilt servo outputs. The STM32F100C8T6B is the IO MCU: it runs px4io firmware and drives the 4 ESC outputs, the 4 control-surface servo outputs (flaperons, ruddervators), the safety switch and the output failsafe (DEC-015, DEC-016, DEC-050, DEC-051).

Pixhawk 6C and Pixhawk 6X are conceptual and functional references. Their schematic and PCB are not copied. The pin allocation follows Pixhawk 6C (`px4/fmu-v6c`) on the H743 and `px4/io-v2` on the F100, by team decision (DEC-014).

### 1.2 Scope

**In scope:**

- H743 main-MCU hardware architecture
- F100 IO MCU hardware: servo PWM, safety switch, servo rail sense and failsafe
- H743↔F100 USART physical interface
- Sensor interfaces: 2 × SPI IMU, 2 × I2C barometer, 1 × I2C magnetometer (DEC-011)
- H743 external interfaces and F100 PWM/expanded-I/O allocation provided by the user
- 5 V flight-controller board input and 6 V servo supply
- USB FS, BOOT0, SWD, reset, protection, testability, PCB, manufacturing, and bring-up requirements
- Verification planning through hardware bring-up

**Out of scope or deferred:**

- PX4 board port (software team: PX4 v1.16.2, based on `px4/fmu-v6c`, DEC-014)
- Bootloader implementation (PX4 bootloader built from the board config, stock px4io bootloader on the F100, DEC-017, DEC-045) and the HSE bypass changes in the H743 board config and the F100 px4io build (DEC-045)
- Firmware image packaging and software flashing implementation
- Detailed USART message protocol and Software-owned behavior
- UBEC model (PWR-011)
- Fabrication release, measured performance, and regulatory approval

## 2. Requirements Management

### 2.1 Writing rules

- Use **shall** for mandatory requirements, **should** for design preferences, **may** for optional capabilities.
- Each requirement has a stable, unique ID. Never reuse an ID after removal.
- Keep requirements separate from implementation choices, assumptions, and verification results.
- A candidate part, pin mapping, resource count, or implementation report is not verification.
- Deferred firmware details are not hardware approval or release evidence.

### 4.3 Power architecture

- Board input and servo supply limits: see PWR-010 and PWR-011.
- The diagram-derived arrangement is a candidate only: 6S battery, XT90-S, PM02D, 5 V, 3.3 V, 3.3 V_IMU, 6 V UBEC.
- No PDB (DEC-054): the PM02D battery output feeds the Tekko32 4in1 ESC battery pads, and the 6 V UBEC is soldered to the same pads. All motor and servo current passes through the PM02D; its current rating against that load is `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- The UBEC plugs into a 1x3 header on each rail: CN804 for `+VSERVO1` and CN805 for `+VSERVO2` (Samtec TSW-101-22-G-T-RA, `conn-actuator`, 4.2; DEC-111). The servo headers of each rail share its + pin and the servo GND bus, so all servo current passes through the board (DEC-019, DEC-110, DEC-113, OPEN-021).
- USB VBUS powers the board on the bench without the battery (IF-022), through power mux IN2 to `+5V` (DEC-029). USB is a bench supply (DEC-038). The PM02D has priority, with the XCOMP fast switchover (DEC-031 to DEC-034).
- 5 V input: TI TPS2121 power mux (DEC-029): IN1 from the PM02D (`PM02_5V`), IN2 from USB `VBUS`, OUT to the `+5V` bus, current limit 1.5 A (DEC-030). TI data in CMP-008 and 4.7. It blocks reverse current on both channels, so neither source back-feeds the other through it (CMP-008). Plan B: the DNP Schottky diodes D105 and D106 replace U101 on a board where U101 is not fitted (DEC-108, OPEN-041).
- Main 3.3 V rail: TPS62142RGTR buck from the 5 V board input (DEC-012).
- Sensor rail: `+3V3A`, from a TPS73533DRBR LDO (DEC-027), feeds the VDD pins of the 2 IMUs, both barometers and the magnetometer (IST8310 AVDD); their VDDIO pins (IST8310 DVDD) stay on `+3V3` (DEC-043).

### 4.4 Clock, reset, boot and programming

- H743 shall retain selectable BOOT0 access (FUN-006). Drawn as push button B902 to `+3V3` with R202 to `GND` (DEC-095); boot-state test in P4.
- H743 and F100 shall provide SWD access (FUN-005).
- USB FS shall be physically available on H743 for later Software-owned flashing/QGC integration.
- H743 clock: 16 MHz HSE, as `px4/fmu-v6c` (DEC-017), from a CMOS oscillator into PH0 (DEC-044), with the HSE in bypass mode (`STM32_HSEBYP_ENABLE` in the board config, DEC-045). No LSE.
- F100 clock: 24 MHz HSE from a CMOS oscillator into PD0 (OSC_IN) (DEC-044), used directly as SYSCLK, as in stock px4io-v2, with the HSE in bypass mode set by a custom px4io build (DEC-017, DEC-045; PX4 v1.16.2 `boards/px4/io-v2/nuttx-config/include/board.h`). The F100 HSE crystal range is 4 to 24 MHz (DocID16455 Rev 9 Table 21, OPEN-020); U302 meets the external-clock (bypass) limits of DocID16455 Rev 9 Table 19 (CMP-010; margins accepted in DEC-089). The stock px4io bootloader runs from HSI. No LSE; PC14 and PC15 unconnected (px4io reads them at boot).
- F100 boot: BOOT0 pulled down (R307 10 kΩ); BOOT1 (PB2) has no resistor, because it is ignored while BOOT0 = 0 (DEC-095). The software team flashes the px4io bootloader over SWD once; after that PX4 updates the F100 over the USART link. No BOOT0/NRST control from the H743.
- H743 first flash: PX4 bootloader over SWD (software team, ST-LINK); updates over USB.
- Boot-time outputs: every PWM line low before firmware starts, with a weak pull-down and no pull-up (team, pinmap review Q10). 100 kΩ to `GND` on all 15 PWM lines, MAIN 1 to 8, AUX 1 to 4, SPARE 1 to 3, as drawn (DEC-069, DEC-073; 4.2 actuator and peripheral connectors rows).
- Watchdogs: px4io starts the F100 IWDG; PX4 v1.16.2 does not start the H743 IWDG (team answer S1).
- Reset: H743 NRST (`H7_RESET_B`) and F100 NRST (`F1_RESET_B`) each join `RESET_B` through a Schottky diode (D201, D301 BAT54XV2T1G), with one reset button B901 (DEC-074, amends DEC-046). B901 resets both MCUs; an internal reset of one MCU does not reset the other. Bench check open (OPEN-026, P4-002).
